📑 Before Visiting the Service Center

To ensure a smooth repair process and protect your privacy, follow this checklist before handing over your device:

Backup Everything

Connect to a PC or use Google One to back up photos and WhatsApp. Service centers often factory reset devices.

Remove Cards

Don't forget to take out your SIM card and any external storage/SD cards.

Carry Your Invoice

For in-warranty repairs, a digital or physical copy of your original purchase invoice is mandatory.

Note the IMEI Number

Dial *#06# and write down your IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 numbers for your records before handing it over.
