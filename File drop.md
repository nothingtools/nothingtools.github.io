## 🔒 Why Use Our File Drop Over Other Apps?

Most file-sharing services (like WeTransfer or Google Drive) require you to upload your file to *their* servers first, and then the receiver downloads it. This takes double the time and compromises your privacy.

Our **File Drop** uses advanced **WebRTC technology**.
* **Zero Servers in the Middle:** Your files travel directly from your Nothing Phone to the receiver's device.
* **No Size Limits:** Want to send a 5GB 4K video shot on your Phone (2a)? Go for it. There are no artificial caps.
* **Uncompressed Quality:** Perfect for sharing raw `.dng` photos, high-res KLWP themes, or heavy firmware `.zip` files.
* **Total Anonymity:** We don't ask for an email, we don't require a login, and we don't store your files. The moment the transfer is done, the connection is wiped.

## 🛠️ How to Use the P2P File Drop

It is incredibly simple and works across all devices (Android, iOS, Windows, Mac) as long as you have a modern web browser.

### Step 1: Initiate the Transfer
1. Open the **[Nothing Space Hub](https://nothingtools.github.io/)** on the sending device.
2. Scroll down to the **File Drop (P2P)** section.
3. Select the file(s) you want to share from your gallery or file manager.

### Step 2: Share the Connection
1. Once the file is selected, the tool will generate a unique, secure link or QR code.
2. Share this link with the receiver (you can WhatsApp or text it to them).

### Step 3: Direct Streaming
1. The receiver opens the link on their device.
2. Ensure both devices keep the browser tab open! 
3. The file will start streaming directly between the two devices at maximum Wi-Fi/Data speeds. Once it hits 100%, the file is saved locally on the receiver's end.
