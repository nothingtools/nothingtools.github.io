🛠️ Step-by-Step Setup Guide

Step 1: Get the Prerequisites

  1. Install KLWP Live Wallpaper Maker from Play Store.
  2. Download your favorite .klwp Depth Effect file from the gallery above.

Step 2: Configure KLWP

  1. Open KLWP app and grant storage/location permissions.
  2. Tap the menu icon and select Load Preset.
  3. Browse internal storage and select the downloaded .klwp file.

Step 3: Apply to Lock Screen

  1. Tap the Save icon (floppy disk) at the top right.
  2. Choose Home screen and lock screen.
  3. Enjoy your new 3D Depth Effect!
