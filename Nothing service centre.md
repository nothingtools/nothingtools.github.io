## 📑 What to Do Before Visiting the Service Center

To ensure a smooth repair process and protect your privacy, follow this checklist before handing over your device:

* **Backup Everything:** Connect your phone to a PC or use Google One to back up your photos, contacts, and WhatsApp chats. Service centers often factory reset devices during testing.
* **Remove SIM & SD Cards:** Don't forget to take out your SIM card and any external storage.
* **Carry Your Invoice:** For in-warranty repairs (usually 1 year for devices, 6 months for accessories), a digital or physical copy of your original purchase invoice (from Flipkart, Myntra, etc.) is mandatory.
* **Note the IMEI Number:** Dial `*#06#` and write down your IMEI 1 and IMEI 2 numbers for your records.
