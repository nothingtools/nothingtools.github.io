Loved a setup from the gallery above? Here is a general guide on how to transform your stock phone into a customized masterpiece:

  1. Hide the Status Bar: The cleanest setups hide the top battery and Wi-Fi icons. You can do this by using a third-party launcher like Nova Launcher Prime or Lawnchair.
  2. Apply the Nothing Icon Pack: Download the official Nothing Icon Pack from the Play Store. Apply it globally via your launcher settings to ensure every app turns into a uniform monochrome dot-matrix style.
  3. Use KWGT for Widgets: The stock widgets are great, but for custom placement, install KWGT (Kustom Widget Maker). Many community members share their custom .kwgt files that perfectly mimic the Nothing style with added weather or music info.
  4. Depth Effect Wallpapers: For the lock screens where the image overlaps the clock, you will need KLWP (Kustom Live Wallpaper). Download the required .klwp themes directly from our hub.
