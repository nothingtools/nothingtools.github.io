🌟 What Are AI Prompts?

If you use advanced AI Image Generators (like Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, or DALL-E), you know that the text input matters heavily. An AI Prompt is a highly detailed set of instructions created to guide the AI's generation process.

When you use these meticulously crafted texts, it instantly adjusts the output's lighting parameters, camera angles, textures, and mood to perfectly match a professional studio shoot.

🛠️ Why Use Custom Prompts?

Cinematic Lighting

Ensure perfect high-contrast studio setups, rim lighting, and directional highlights.

Ultra-Realistic Details

Achieve a DSLR-like texture quality with flawless facial features and clothing materials.

Moody & Introspective

Direct the exact pose, expression, and background vibe (like film noir) for dramatic effects.

Reference Consistency

Keep the subject's face identical across different environments using reference images.

📥 How to Use These Styles

  1. Browse the Gallery section above to find a style you like.
  2. Note the name of the style (e.g., "Thinker Portrait").
  3. Click the button below to visit the Nothing Space Hub.
  4. Find the exact text prompt for your chosen style and copy it.
  5. If the tool supports reference images (like Midjourney's --cref), upload your source face image.
  6. Hit generate and enjoy your cinematic masterpiece!
