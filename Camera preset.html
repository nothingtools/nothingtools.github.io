🌟 What Are Camera Presets (XMLs)?

If you use advanced camera ports (like Google Camera/GCam) on your Nothing device, you know that the default settings aren't always perfect. An XML Camera Preset is a pre-configured settings file created by community developers.

When you load this file, it instantly adjusts hundreds of hidden camera parameters (like noise reduction, HDR+ frames, saturation, and edge detection) to perfectly match your specific Nothing Phone's lens architecture.

🛠️ Why You Need Custom Presets

Perfect Skin Tones

Eliminate the artificial "whitening" or over-saturation often found in stock image processing.

Cinematic Portraits

Achieve a DSLR-like natural bokeh (background blur) with flawless edge detection around hair and glasses.

Nightscape & Astro

Capture the Milky Way with specially tuned long-exposure presets optimized for low-light sensors.

Macro Brilliance

Get incredibly sharp close-ups and details without the image falling apart or getting noisy.

📥 How to Apply XML Presets on Your Device

  1. Visit the Playground section on Nothing Space.
  2. Download the .xml preset file designed for your specific phone model (e.g., Phone 2a vs Phone 1).
  3. Open your File Manager and navigate to your internal storage.
  4. Create a folder structure for your specific camera app (For GCam, it's usually GCam/Configs8 or GCam/Configs9).
  5. Move the downloaded .xml file into this new folder.
  6. Open your camera app, double-tap the black area next to the shutter button, and select your preset from the dropdown to import it. The app will restart with the new optimized settings!
