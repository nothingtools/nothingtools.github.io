💡 How to Apply Without Auto-Zoom

Android sometimes auto-zooms wallpapers, ruining the composition. Here is the fix:

  1. Download your image from Nothing Space.
  2. Go to Settings > Customisation > Wallpaper & style.
  3. Select your image and use two fingers to pinch inwards slightly before hitting apply. This ensures the full image fits the screen!
