💡 How to Sync with Glyph Interface

Once you've downloaded your perfect .mp3 or .ogg track from Nothing Space, here is how to make your Nothing Phone's lights react to it:

  1. Open Settings on your Nothing Phone.
  2. Navigate to Glyph Interface > Ringtones.
  3. Under 'Default ringtone', tap on Add a ringtone (or select internal storage).
  4. Browse to your Downloads folder and select the audio file you got from Nothing Space.
  5. The Magic Step: Nothing OS will automatically analyze the beats, bass, and treble of the track and generate a custom light show for the Glyph LEDs!

Pro-Tip for the Best Experience

Tracks with heavy bass drops, clear drum beats, and sharp electronic synths produce the best lighting effects. Avoid acoustic or ambient tracks if you want a highly active LED pattern.
